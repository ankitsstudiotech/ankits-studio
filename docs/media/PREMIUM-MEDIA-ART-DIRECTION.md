# Premium Media Art Direction

**Stage:** 4A — art direction target for synthetic preview and later real intake  
**Policy:** `SYNTHETIC-MEDIA-PREVIEW-POLICY.md`

---

## Design read

Indian neighbourhood **boutique** fitness / dance studio. Editorial sports photography. Cinematic available light. Authentic movement. Realistic bodies and studio scale. Premium dark grading with human warmth. Energetic without looking like a nightclub.

---

## Visual character

- Compact modern movement rooms — mats, open floor, modest mirrors, simple equipment
- Adults who look like neighbourhood professionals and parents — not influencer castings
- Warm natural sidelight / late-day window light; restrained accent spill at most
- Slight filmic contrast; deep field blacks consistent with Studio Pulse (`#0b0b0c` chrome)
- Motion implied in stance, weight shift, or mid-phrase — not frozen “hero pose” clichés
- Anonymous subjects only in synthetic work

---

## Avoid

- Giant commercial gym machines / luxury health-club atriums
- Bodybuilder or extreme physique stereotypes
- Influencer fitness clichés, neon cyberpunk, excessive purple lighting
- Floating particles, fake text / logos / signage
- Impossible mirrored rooms, overly symmetrical AI compositions
- Everyone looking into camera; perfect plastic skin
- Fake before/after imagery; embedded typography
- Overly American / European boutique-gym branding context
- Imagery that could be mistaken for a named Airoli / Ghansoli / Thane branch

---

## Plausibility bar

Scenes must feel credible for a **Navi Mumbai / Thane neighbourhood studio** scale — not a destination mega-gym.

Synthetic imagery still must **not** be presented as an actual physical branch.

---

## Relationship to Stage 3 motion

Media is subordinate to typography.

- H1 remains primary on heroes
- Media may reveal in parallel or slightly after brand — never delay headline choreography
- Allowed treatments: hero reveal, section reveal, subtle hover crop on discovery media, mobile static / tap-safe
- Forbidden: constant Ken Burns, parallax spam, scroll-jacking, 3D tilt, magnetic cursor, image wobble, random clip-paths
