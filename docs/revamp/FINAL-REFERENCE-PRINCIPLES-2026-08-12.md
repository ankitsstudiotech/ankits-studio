# Final reference principles — 12 August 2026

Owner reference sites inspected for **principles only** — not cloned layout, copy, pricing, or claims.

Sites: [Cult.fit](https://www.cult.fit/) · [Orangetheory India](https://www.orangetheory.com/en-in) · [Rhythm and Flow](https://rhythmandflow.in/)

---

## Cult.fit (3 principles)

1. **First-screen clarity** — service category and primary action are obvious within seconds.
2. **Service discovery density** — multiple programmes are scannable without identical card walls.
3. **Strong CTA hierarchy** — one dominant action; secondary paths stay quieter.

**Do not copy:** national scale tone, app-first funnels, their pricing/offers, or franchise breadth.

---

## Orangetheory (3 principles)

1. **Energy through typography and contrast** — bold headlines carry motion without gimmicky animation.
2. **Programme-led storytelling** — each service has a distinct personality in copy and rhythm.
3. **Premium restraint** — dark + accent palette feels focused, not cluttered.

**Do not copy:** heart-rate/performance guarantees, US franchise claims, orange brand swap, or calorie-burn language.

---

## Rhythm and Flow (3 principles)

1. **Local studio warmth** — neighbourhood context and approachability in hero/support copy.
2. **Light chapter contrast** — editorial sections break dark/heavy layouts for readability.
3. **Human founder/story presence** — compact credibility without testimonial theatre.

**Do not copy:** their single-location model, class schedule tables we do not have data for, or unrelated dance-genre positioning.
