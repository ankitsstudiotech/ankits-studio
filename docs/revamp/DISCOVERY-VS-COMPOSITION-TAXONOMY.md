# Discovery taxonomy vs composition families

_12 August 2026 — final owner visual architecture._

## Two different concepts

| Layer | Purpose | Values |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery taxonomy** | Homepage + `/programs` index grouping | TRAIN · MOVE · CELEBRATE · **FOR TEAMS** |
| **Composition families** (Stage 5) | Programme detail page layout grammar | structured · fluid · calm · **service** |

They are intentionally separate.

## Corporate Wellness

- **Discovery:** `serviceCluster: "teams"` → customer-facing label **For Teams**
- **Detail page:** `composeFamilyFromSlug("corporate-wellness")` → **service** (same family as Wedding Choreography and Home/Online PT)
- **Motion tone:** `direct` (workplace rhythm — not ceremonial wedding tone)

Wedding Choreography remains the sole **Celebrate** discovery cluster member.

## Why

Corporate Wellness is not a wedding/occasion service. Grouping it under Celebrate was a Prompt 1 migration error. The owner’s eighth service needed a fourth discovery path without inventing a fifth composition system.
