## Third Space (London luxury clubs)

**What works:** Acquisition site separated from member app; modular mobile-first UI; clear CTAs; club discovery with map/filter friction reduction; micro-animations for expandable learning, not endless scroll theatre.  
**Why:** Matches a real job (join a club) instead of decorating a brochure.  
**Applicable:** Treat Ankit’s site as **acquisition + trial**, not a member portal. Invest in branch discovery as a first-class journey.  
**Must not copy:** Ultra-luxury club pricing posture; London lifestyle photography vocabulary; membership-app patterns.  
**Principle:** *Separate acquisition storytelling from member operations; make location choice a designed system.*

## Equinox

**What works:** Near-zero UI chrome; full-bleed editorial photography; compressed display type; black canvas hierarchy that subordinates interface to aspiration imagery.  
**Why:** Brand authority comes from image + type scale, not card grids.  
**Applicable:** Let photography/type carry first-viewport authority; shrink chrome.  
**Must not copy:** Exclusive luxury signalling that conflicts with neighbourhood family studio; body-aesthetic aspiration that can alienate yoga/kids audiences.  
**Principle:** *Authority through crop and type scale, not more UI widgets.*

## 1Rebel

**What works:** Mobile-first rebuild; schedule/instructor discovery tied to multi-location reality; energetic visual system matching studio intensity; performance/SEO treated as product.  
**Why:** Boutique energy without losing operational clarity.  
**Applicable:** Programme/timetable discovery must feel native to phone users; energy in UI should match class tempo, not random bounce.  
**Must not copy:** Nightclub lighting as literal theme; city-scale multi-country IA.  
**Principle:** *Match interface energy to studio tempo while keeping schedule/branch jobs crisp.*

## Barry’s

**What works:** Signature environment (Red Room) drives colour/system; immersive, high-intensity layouts; community + conversion integrated.  
**Why:** One spatial metaphor organises the whole digital language.  
**Applicable:** Ankit needs a metaphor that holds strength *and* dance — not a single red-room clone.  
**Must not copy:** Red-room monoculture; HIIT-only branding.  
**Principle:** *One spatial metaphor can organise colour and motion — only if it fits the whole programme mix.*

## BXR London

**What works (from category peers):** Performance-club clarity; equipment/story photography; restrained premium without spa softness.  
**Applicable:** Strength credibility via material/photo language.  
**Must not copy:** Boxing-only identity.  
**Principle:** *Material truth (floor, iron, sweat, fabric) beats generic stock fitness smiles.*

## Phive Clubs (Awwwards SOTD 2025)

**What works:** Timetable as a designed object; class modals; responsive type animation; scroll choreography with club storytelling.  
**Why:** Classes and clubs are first-class interactive surfaces.  
**Applicable:** Timetable and programme selection deserve signature interaction — carefully, without hijacking scroll for Ankit’s a11y/perf constraints.  
**Must not copy:** Award-site scroll hijack density; heavy JS as default.  
**Principle:** *Make class/branch discovery interactive theatre only where it clarifies choice.*

## School of Motion

**What works (owner-supplied benchmark):** Motion used to teach craft; editorial pacing; animation with explanatory purpose.  
**Why:** Motion is content, not garnish.  
**Applicable:** Use motion to explain programme tempo / studio energy; teach the brand.  
**Must not copy:** Education-product IA; course-catalogue patterns.  
**Principle:** *Every major motion should explain something about movement or place.*

## La Huella / Essor / Fitonist (Awwwards fitness peers)

**What works:** Immersive club culture pages; high-contrast systems; scroll-linked storytelling.  
**Applicable:** Culture/story sections can break marketing-stack monotony.  
**Must not copy:** AI-app product positioning (Fitonist); scroll-jacking that fails reduced-motion.  
**Principle:** *Break catalogue monotony with one culture/story surface, not eight identical feature bands.*

## cult.fit (Aurora design language) — Indian multi-discipline fitness

**What works:** Bold/energetic principles; intent-driven (“does it solve core intent?”); systems thinking at scale for many disciplines.  
**Why:** Proves multi-offering fitness can share one language if hierarchy is ruthless.  
**Applicable:** Intent filters for programme choice; mobile density.  
**Must not copy:** Aurora glassmorphism + dynamic brand-mesh backgrounds (perf risk; AI-adjacent glow risk); marketplace gamification.  
**Principle:** *Intent-first multi-discipline systems — without glassmorphism as a crutch.*

## The Danceworx (Mumbai/Delhi dance academy)

**What works:** Style-led exploration (ballet, jazz, contemporary…); studio locator; adult + kids coexistence without SaaS card grids as the only metaphor.  
**Applicable:** Programme browsing as craft styles; family audiences.  
**Must not copy:** Academy clipart density; unverified vanity stats presentation.  
**Principle:** *Programme as craft discipline, not feature tile.*

## Consolidated principles (max 10)

1. **Acquisition, not member app** — Primary jobs are discover → trust → trial.  
2. **Place is a product surface** — Branch choice needs designed discovery, not equal cards.  
3. **Type + crop before chrome** — Authority from typography and media, fewer soft cards.  
4. **One coherent metaphor for many motions** — Strength and dance share a world without gym or spa cliché.  
5. **Tempo-matched energy** — Visual/motion intensity should vary by programme family.  
6. **Fewer layout families** — Break the repeating eyebrow-title-grid stack.  
7. **Motion that explains** — Animate to teach tempo/place/state; not to decorate every section.  
8. **Mobile composition, not stacked desktop** — Phone gets a different lockup and order.  
9. **Honesty over aspiration porn** — No invented luxury proof; mock media must be art-directed placeholders.  
10. **Performance & reduced-motion as craft** — Award-site density is not the default; interruptible, purposeful motion only.

These principles feed Phase 3 directions A/B/C without copying any single brand’s layout.
