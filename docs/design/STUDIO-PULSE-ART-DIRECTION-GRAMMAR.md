# Studio Pulse — Art-direction grammar

Tokens stay shared. Composition is allowed to vary.

## Tokens (shared — do not fork)

- Colour field / ink / accents (strength, calm, high-energy, warm)
- Typography: Bebas Neue display · Space Grotesk body/UI
- Spacing scale, gutters, container widths
- CTA language and sticky trial behaviour
- Motion tokens + Stage 3 primitives
- Media model + synthetic safety flag
- Accessibility (contrast, focus, 44px targets, reduced motion)

## Composition (variable)

Four programme **composition families** (not seven page forks):

| Family | Slugs | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **A Structured** | Functional Training | Architectural grid, decisive rules, tall media, compact meta rail |
| **B Fluid / expressive** | Zumba, Dance | Asymmetric title/media; Zumba tighter; Dance more open / media breaks grid |
| **C Calm** | Yoga | Breathing room; media lower/right; quiet typographic facts |
| **D Service / occasion** | Wedding, Home PT, Online | Delivery-story first; intimate or ceremonial; compact remote |

Homepage, About, Locations, and utility routes use the same tokens with **route-appropriate** compositions (editorial vs conversion-first).

## Principles

### A. Asymmetry with alignment
Prefer 5/7, 7/5, 4/8, offset pairs. Media may break the content boundary selectively. Avoid arbitrary centering of hero content.

### B. Scale contrast
Use hero display, section display, utility heading, and micro-label deliberately. Not every `h2` shares the same optical weight.

### C. Editorial continuity
Lead sections with rules, alignment, media edges, numbering, accent lines, and negative space — not isolated “blocks”.

### D. Controlled interruption
Each major page may have **one** surprising compositional moment (grid-breaking media, large number, vertical rail, offset facts). Other sections stay calm.

### E. Content determines composition
No fake empty panels. Thin content stays thin. Process steps only when verified in content.

### F. Fewer boxes
Prefer type, dividers, rails, whitespace. Bordered cards only for real grouping (forms, controls, genuine clusters).

## Rule / divider language

| Role | Form | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Section divider** | Full-bleed 1px `border-on-field` | Separates major page bands |
| **Accent rule** | Short horizontal (~3.5–4rem), accent-tinted | Marks start of a hero/support thought |
| **Vertical rail** | 2px accent or muted edge | Aligns principle lists / meta columns |
| **Programme cue** | Segmented motion cue on rows | Discovery energy only — not decorative page chrome |

Do not mix arbitrary rule lengths without one of these roles.

## Glance / facts presentation

Retire universal “At a glance” + identical bordered panels.

Prefer family-appropriate labels (e.g. session snapshot, what to expect, training at your location) and typographic treatments: 2-col facts, horizontal meta strip, large number + label, thin separators.

## Utility routes

Pricing, timetable, trial, contact stay conversion-first — intentional splits, not programme editorial theatre. No synthetic imagery required.
